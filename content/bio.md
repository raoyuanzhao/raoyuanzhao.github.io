I am a PhD student and research associate at [**LMU Munich**](https://www.lmu.de/en/), advised by [**Dr. Michael A. Hedderich**](https://michael-hedderich.de/). I am part of the Center for Information and Language Processing (CIS), co-led by [**Prof. Hinrich Schütze**](https://cisnlp.github.io/) and [**Prof. Barbara Plank**](https://bplank.github.io/), and affiliated with [**MaiNLP**](https://mainlp.github.io/) and [**MCML**](https://mcml.ai/).

My research interests include **synthetic data generation, reinforcement learning for LLM post-training, model evaluation, multilingual NLP, and LLM reasoning**. I also have broader interests in the intersection of language technology, culture, and literature.

I am currently a Research Intern at [**Cohere**](https://cohere.com/), working on instruction following and self-verification in large language models.
