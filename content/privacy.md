See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

Fonts are bundled with the site, so no requests are made to font providers.

## Visitor statistics

The sidebar shows a world map with the number of visits from each country. To count visits, this site uses [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), an open-source analytics service that does not use cookies.

When you open a page, a small script from GoatCounter (gc.zgo.at) sends the page address (without query string), the page title, the referring page, and your screen size to GoatCounter. As with any web request, your IP address, browser user agent, and language settings are transmitted too. GoatCounter uses the IP address only to determine your country and to avoid counting repeated page views within a few hours as new visits. It does not store IP addresses, does not store anything in your browser (no cookies or local storage), and does not track you across websites. See the [GoatCounter privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy) for details.

Nothing is counted if your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. The map and figures in the sidebar contain only aggregated numbers per country, refreshed a few times a day. They are part of this website and load nothing from third parties.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in knowing, in aggregated form, where the readers of this academic website come from (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by enabling Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control in your browser, or by blocking the script.
